About me

I'm Mara — an illustrator working primarily in ink and gouache, with occasional excursions into the digital. I came to illustration through a literature degree and a stack of secondhand art books, which probably explains why most of my work ends up looking like it belongs inside one.

I've worked with magazines, independent publishers, and brands who want imagery that feels considered rather than quick — the kind of picture that rewards a second look. I'm particularly drawn to commissions where the subject has some texture to it: memory, place, natural history, the quiet strangeness of daily life.

I'm based in Amsterdam and typically take on three to four commissions a month. If you have a project in mind, I'd love to hear about it.
