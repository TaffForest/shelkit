From the Beacons
to the stage.

Carys Meredith grew up in a small village on the edge of the Brecon Beacons, learning Welsh folk tunes on the clàrsach before she could read sheet music. Her relationship with the landscape is inseparable from her songwriting — every melody seems to carry the weight of slate hills and the sound of water over stone.

After studying at the Royal Welsh College of Music & Drama, she spent two years living on the Llŷn Peninsula, writing what would become her debut record, Llechen Las (Blue Slate). The album was recorded live to tape in a converted chapel in Pontypridd and released to quiet, committed acclaim.

She now performs across the UK and Europe, accompanied only by her harp, her voice, and — on occasion — a rotating cast of friends she refuses to call a backing band.
