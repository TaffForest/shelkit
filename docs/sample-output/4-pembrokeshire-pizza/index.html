    Our story

    Built on good ingredients & bad weather.

    We started with a wood-fired oven, a lease on a building that smelled permanently of sea salt, and a conviction that Pembrokeshire had everything a great pizza needed — it just needed someone to put it all together.

    Our dough proofs for 48 hours. Our mozzarella comes from a dairy outside Haverfordwest. The laverbread is from the Gower, a short drive up the coast. The lamb is as local as it gets.

    We're open seven days. Come in from the rain.
